Which products are available?

The business lists herbal shampoo and herbal hair oil. Contact the team for current varieties, sizes, prices and stock.

Can I place an order here?

You can prepare a product enquiry and choose to send it through WhatsApp or email. An enquiry is not an order confirmation, and this concept website does not take payments.

Yes—use the enquiry form to ask about retail or salon supply. The team can confirm whether wholesale arrangements, minimum quantities and delivery options are available.
